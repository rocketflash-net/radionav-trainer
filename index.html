Materi singkat

Radial, QDM, QDR

Radial adalah garis magnetik yang keluar dari VOR. Radial 090 berada di timur stasiun.

  • QDR = bearing magnetik dari stasiun ke pesawat (setara radial, dipakai untuk NDB).
  • QDM = bearing magnetik dari pesawat ke stasiun. QDM = QDR ± 180.
  • Terbang inbound di radial 090 berarti track 270 (QDM 270).

Membaca HSI

  • Panah magenta = course yang dipilih (CRS/OBS). Batang tengah = CDI.
  • Fly toward the needle: jika CDI di kanan, course ada di kanan pesawat.
  • Skala: VOR 5° per dot (2 dot = 10°). LOC 1,25° per dot.
  • Segitiga TO/FROM menunjukkan apakah course yang dipilih menuju atau menjauhi stasiun.
  • Untuk inbound set CRS = track inbound (flag TO). Untuk outbound set CRS = radial (flag FROM).

Langkah intercept

  1. Orientasi. Saya di radial berapa? Lihat ekor jarum RMI, atau putar OBS sampai CDI di tengah dengan FROM.
  2. Set course yang diminta.
  3. Pilih heading intercept = course ± sudut intercept, ke arah sisi jarum CDI.
  4. Lead the turn. Saat CDI mulai bergerak ke tengah, mulai belok ke course.
  5. Tracking. Koreksi angin, pertahankan CDI di tengah.

Memilih sudut intercept

  • Inbound: sudut intercept = 2 × selisih sudut (minimal 20°, maksimal 90°). Selisih radial 20° → intercept 40°.
  • Outbound: pakai 30°–45°. Makin jauh dari stasiun, radial makin renggang dan intercept terasa lambat.
  • Dekat stasiun, radial sangat rapat: CDI bergerak cepat, jadi lead turn lebih awal.

NDB dengan RMI

  • Kepala jarum = QDM. Ekor jarum = QDR.
  • Inbound: established saat kepala jarum menunjuk QDM yang diminta dan heading ≈ QDM (tanpa angin).
  • Outbound: established saat ekor jarum menunjuk QDR yang diminta.
  • Ingat: push the head, pull the tail. Saat intercept, kepala jarum bergerak menjauhi heading; ekor jarum bergerak mendekati.

ILS approach

  • LOC memberi panduan lateral, GS panduan vertikal (umumnya 3°).
  • ATC biasanya memberi vektor dengan sudut intercept LOC ≤ 30°.
  • Intercept GS dari bawah: pertahankan ketinggian sampai diamond GS turun ke tengah.
  • Rate turun ≈ groundspeed × 5 fpm. Tinggi di GS ≈ 300 ft per NM.
  • CAT I: di DA 200 ft, LOC dan GS harus di dalam 1 dot, kalau tidak lakukan missed approach.

Koreksi angin: wind correction angle ≈ (crosswind ÷ TAS) × 60. Simulator ini menyederhanakan banyak hal (variasi magnetik, slant range DME, kecepatan belok tetap 3°/detik). Dipakai untuk memahami konsep, bukan pengganti latihan dengan instruktur atau simulator tersertifikasi.

Quick lessons

Radial, QDM, QDR

A radial is a magnetic line going out from a VOR. Radial 090 lies east of the station.

  • QDR = magnetic bearing from the station to the aircraft (same idea as a radial, used for NDBs).
  • QDM = magnetic bearing from the aircraft to the station. QDM = QDR ± 180.
  • Flying inbound on radial 090 means a track of 270 (QDM 270).

Reading the HSI

  • Magenta arrow = selected course (CRS/OBS). Center bar = CDI.
  • Fly toward the needle: if the CDI is to the right, the course is to your right.
  • Scale: VOR 5° per dot (2 dots = 10°). LOC 1.25° per dot.
  • The TO/FROM triangle shows whether the selected course leads to or away from the station.
  • Inbound: set CRS to the inbound track (TO flag). Outbound: set CRS to the radial (FROM flag).

Intercept steps

  1. Orient. Which radial am I on? Check the RMI needle tail, or turn the OBS until the CDI centers with FROM.
  2. Set the course you were given.
  3. Pick an intercept heading = course ± intercept angle, toward the side the CDI is on.
  4. Lead the turn. When the CDI starts moving to the center, start turning onto the course.
  5. Track. Correct for wind and keep the CDI centered.

Choosing the intercept angle

  • Inbound: intercept angle = 2 × the angle off course (at least 20°, at most 90°). 20° off → 40° intercept.
  • Outbound: use 30°–45°. Far from the station the radials spread out, so the intercept feels slow.
  • Close to the station the radials are tight: the CDI moves fast, so lead the turn earlier.

NDB with the RMI

  • Needle head = QDM. Needle tail = QDR.
  • Inbound: you are established when the head points at the given QDM and your heading ≈ QDM (no wind).
  • Outbound: you are established when the tail points at the given QDR.
  • Remember: push the head, pull the tail. During an intercept the head moves away from your heading and the tail moves toward it.

ILS approach

  • The LOC gives lateral guidance, the GS gives vertical guidance (usually 3°).
  • ATC normally vectors you to intercept the LOC at 30° or less.
  • Intercept the GS from below: hold altitude until the GS diamond comes down to the center.
  • Descent rate ≈ groundspeed × 5 fpm. Height on the GS ≈ 300 ft per NM.
  • CAT I: at the 200 ft DA, LOC and GS must be within 1 dot. If not, fly the missed approach.

Wind correction: wind correction angle ≈ (crosswind ÷ TAS) × 60. This simulator keeps things simple (no magnetic variation, no DME slant range, fixed 3°/second turn rate). Use it to understand the concepts. It does not replace training with an instructor or a certified simulator.
